# Third-party public tennis data

## PredixSport tennis ratings

Source: https://www.predixsport.com/
Methodology/license: https://www.predixsport.com/methodology

The Tennis Truth Engine public-evidence adapter is designed to read publicly published ATP/WTA rating information from PredixSport pages only when the page itself identifies the data as CC BY 4.0.

License: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0).
Attribution: Tennis ELO ratings by PredixSport.

The adapter does not copy or bundle a third-party database in this repository. It retrieves explicitly published public observations and records the source URL, retrieval time, license and attribution with the evidence.

Do not add a dataset to the production evidence store unless its commercial reuse terms have been verified. Non-commercial datasets must not be bundled into the commercial production path.
