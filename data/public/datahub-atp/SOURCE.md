# DataHub ATP World Tour tennis data
Source: https://datahub.io/core/atp-world-tour-tennis-data
Upstream: https://github.com/serve-and-volley/atp-world-tour-tennis-data
License: CC BY 4.0.
Included: ATP scores and detailed statistics 1991-2017.
IMPORTANT: this is an ARCHIVAL source ending in 2017. Refreshing the files does not make the observations current. It may support historical/reconstructed evidence only and must never be represented as current player form.
